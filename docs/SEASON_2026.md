# Chromies 2026 — season record from the paper scorebooks

Transcribed from 35 photographed sheets (32 distinct games; three were shot
twice). Scores are read from the circled result, which the scorekeeper writes
**us-first**: a circled `W` means the Chromies are the larger number, a circled
`L` the smaller. Where a sheet has RUNS rows they are runs-this-inning /
running-total pairs, and every legible one reconciles with the circled result.

`D1`/`D2`/`D3` are weekends one, two and three. A fourth book carries no
weekend label; its games are listed here as **W4** pending confirmation.
